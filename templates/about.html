Synthetic data. Real engineering.

The bundled dataset includes 12 brands, 36 models, and 10 example cities. Prices follow realistic depreciation relationships: newer cars generally cost more, while distance and additional owners reduce value. Brand, trim specifications, fuel, transmission, and location also influence the generated price.

Reference specifications are approximate. This demo uses USD throughout and does not reproduce a particular country’s live vehicle market. Price ranges use the 90th percentile of absolute test residuals, so they are illustrative rather than calibrated confidence intervals.
